- Main Details -

Ice hockey is a sport. In the above picture the boxes labled "1." are the penalty boxes. That is where the players go to serve the penalty. The boxes labled "2." are the team benches. When the players aren't on the ice playing (or in the penalty box) they are usually on the bench, resting for the next shift. The red dotted line in the center of the ice is called the center line. The dots are called face-off dots

- Players and Positioning -

In regular play there can only be a total of 6 player on the ice. A goalie's main job is to stop the other team from scoring. Goalies have to stay on their half of the ice, and can only play the puck ( - interact with the puck using the stick - ) in the crease or in the trapazoid behind the net. The crease is the blue paint in front of the net, and the trapazoid is created by red lines. A forward's main job is to score goals. Forwards can play anywhere on the ice, and there are three of them.

- Positioning -

Forwards:
~ Right Wing (Normally lines up on the right side of the circle at a face-off.)
~ Left Wing (Normally lines up on the left side of a circle at a face-off.)
~ Center (Normally lines up in the circle at a faceoff.)

Defence:
~ Right Defence (Normally lines up behind and to the right of the circle at a face-off.)
~ Left Defence (Normally lines up behind and to the left of the circle at a face-off.)
~ Goalie (Goalies are the last line of defence, but are a unique position. Normally stays by the net.)
